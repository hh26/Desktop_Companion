================================================================================
                    WELCOME TO DISPLAY BUDDY! 🐾✨
             Your Personal Animated Desktop Companion
================================================================================

QUICK START (5 SECONDS):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Double-click "DisplayBuddy.exe" (or "Start_DisplayBuddy.bat").
2. Your companion will wake up and start walking happily along your taskbar!


HOW TO PLAY & INTERACT:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• CLICK HIM:
  Click on your companion anytime to hear what's on his mind!

• DRAG & DROP:
  Click and hold with the left mouse button to pick him up and move him anywhere
  on your screen! When you let go, he falls back down with realistic physics.
  Sometimes, he'll comically hang suspended in mid-air!

• RIGHT-CLICK MENU:
  Right-click on him anytime to open his actions menu:
    - 😘 I want a Kiss... (He will excitedly sprint over to give you sweet cheek kisses!)
    - 🤗 Hug me!!        (He runs over for a warm, cozy embrace!)
    - 🥱 I'm bored.      (Playful banter or quirky jokes)
    - 💬 Say Something   (Quotes, sweet reminders, or fun thoughts)
    - 🎨 Change Outfit   (Switch between stylish color palettes)
    - 🚶 Start Walking   (Get him moving around)
    - ☕ Sit Down        (Let him relax with a warm drink)
    - 💤 Take a Nap      (Tuck him in for some zzz's)

• SYSTEM TRAY ICON (Bottom right near the Windows clock):
  Look for the small companion face icon in your Windows taskbar tray:
    - Reset Position : Brings him back to the front and center of your taskbar.
    - Mute/Unmute    : Toggle sound effects on/off.
    - Exit           : Say goodbye and close the application.


HOW TO CUSTOMIZE QUOTES & NAMES (OPTIONAL):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Want to personalize what he says or change the badge initials?
1. Open the "skins" folder -> "default_companion" folder.
2. Open "dialogues.json" with Notepad (Right-click -> Open with -> Notepad).
3. You can edit:
   - "initials": Change the 1-2 letter person initials beneath each photo badge.
   - "idle_quotes", "click_quotes", "hug_quotes": Add your own inside jokes,
     sweet notes, or reminders!
4. Save the file and restart Display Buddy!


WANT HIM TO START AUTOMATICALLY WHEN WINDOWS TURNS ON? (OPTIONAL):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Right-click "DisplayBuddy.exe" (or "Start_DisplayBuddy.bat") and choose
   "Create shortcut".
2. Press [Windows Key + R] on your keyboard to open the Run dialog.
3. Type: shell:startup   and press Enter.
4. Move or paste the shortcut into that Startup folder.
   Now Display Buddy will greet you every time your PC turns on!


Enjoy having your companion by your side! ❤️
